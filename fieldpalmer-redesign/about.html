Local enough to know. Established enough to deliver.

The operating idea

People helping people through complicated property decisions.

The strongest part of Field Palmer’s public positioning is not a technology claim or a marketing slogan. It is the idea that good estate agency is a specialised customer service, delivered by experienced people who listen, communicate and solve problems.
